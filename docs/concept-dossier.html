Game Design & Architecture Dossier

YAD — Yet Another Dungeon

A 90s retro FPS dungeon crawler pairing scalable 320×200–1280×800 pixel rendering with binary Doom WAD maps and ASCII grids on Small World Machine.

🕹️Genre: 90s FPS Dungeon Crawler
📺Display: 320×200–1280×800 Retro / 256 Colors
⚡Renderer: WebGPU / WebGL2 / WebGL1
📁Level Sources: Doom WAD (E1M1) & ASCII Grid

🎯 Vision & Design Pillars

📺 Selectable CRT & VGA Look

Pillarboxed 16:10 retro display locking the pixel HUD at crisp 320×200 while letting players switch internal 3D render resolution live (320×200, 640×400, 960×600, 1280×800) alongside toggleable QuantizeElement color banding.

🗺️ Dual WAD & ASCII Level Pipeline

Loads authentic binary Doom WADs (e1m1.wad via BSP subsector triangulation, linedef doors, lifts, and exits) as well as plain-text ASCII grids (level1.txt) for instant text-editor prototyping.

🌋 Dynamic Shaders & Arsenal

Pairs retro textures with rippled FluidSurfaceMaterial lava pools, animated PointLight torches, a full 7-weapon arsenal, 6 keycards/skull keys, and 35 Freedoom 0.13.0 spatial SFX plus rendered MIDI soundtracks.

⚔️ Classic Tank & FPS Controls

Switchable retro tank turning or free mouselook FPS navigation with FOV/zoom support, camera/weapon view-bobbing, AABB/sector collision, and immediate spatial audio cues.

⚙️ Technical Engine Integration

Subsystem Components Used Purpose & Function
Camera & Display PerspectiveProjection, CameraStrategyType.FPS, ZoomController Configurable FOV (60°–110°), selectable 320×200–1280×800 render target scaled with pixelated filtering inside a 16:10 320×200 HUD viewport.
WAD & Grid Geometry WadParser, WadLevelBuilder, GridLevelBuilder, ModelGeometry, InstancedMesh Subsector polygon triangulation for Doom WAD maps plus instanced voxel walls and textured floor/ceiling tiles for ASCII grids.
Liquid & Lighting FluidSurfaceMaterial, PointLight, AmbientLight, DirectionalLight Animated wave shader for lava hazards with pulsing torchlight and configurable level brightness.
Post-Processing ToneMappingElement, QuantizeElement ACES filmic exposure control plus toggleable 8-step-per-channel color quantization for 90s DOS color banding.
Physics & Hitbox WadPhysics, BoundingBox, StaticCollider, HazardGrid 2.5D sector/linedef sliding, step-up, and door/lift collision in WAD maps; octree AABB and cell hazard checks in grid levels.
Audio, Weapons & HUD AudioSystem, WeaponView, Hud, SettingsMenu 35 preloaded Freedoom WAVs + .m4a soundtracks, 7 weapons with hitscan/projectile combat, 6 keys, and live Doom-styled settings menu.
Asset Streaming Texture.fromUrl, TextLoader, fetch Loads binary .wad + JSON manifests, ASCII level files, WebP sprite sheets, and local audio assets.
